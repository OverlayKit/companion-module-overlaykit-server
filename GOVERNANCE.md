# Doctrine-Driven Development

This repository develops knowledge in the following order:

```text
Constitution
Discovery
User Story
Architectural Question
Hypothesis
Slice
Review
Evidence
ADR
Next Question
```

An ADR is not a proposal. It crystallizes a bounded result only after a Slice has attempted to
refute its hypothesis and the human authority has accepted the evidence.

The deterministic TypeScript host compiles accepted immutable decisions, the active profile,
specifications, and registered mechanisms into `plan.json`. Change contracts and all normative
inputs are hashed into `manifest.json`. Evidence never changes the law it observes.

Human acceptance of a hypothesis, acceptance of experimental evidence, authorization to merge, and
authorization to publish are separate transitions.
